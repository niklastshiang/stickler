Welcome to Stickler

To hear the drums, Stickler needs two trained models. They are downloaded once; after that, everything runs on this computer, offline.

  • ADTOF · detects the hits Its trained model is free for non-commercial use only (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
    1.3 GB
  • Beat This! · finds the beat and the bars MIT licence. Its model comes from the JKU Linz server.
    0.9 GB
  • Together, on disk about 2.2 GB

By downloading, you accept that ADTOF’s model is used for non-commercial purposes only.
