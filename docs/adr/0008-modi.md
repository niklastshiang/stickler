# ADR 0008: Two modes, Fast and Quality

- Status: accepted; both modes kept unchanged in release 7 (owner, 2026-11-17)
- Date: 2026-11-17 (decisions since 2026-11-12)

## Context
Stickler's benchmark compares separators and transcribers on three datasets: MDB Drums (23
tracks), ENST-Drums (64 tracks) and 25 of the owner's own songs with references (mostly rock and
metal, the music Stickler is for). The plan expected one winning pipeline, recorded as
`docs/adr/0005-gewinner-pipeline.md` (Checkpoint C). That number went to YourMT3+'s ADR instead,
and the owner chose two modes rather than one winner. This ADR records that choice and its
history, and the decision on the release 7 candidates.

Macro-F1 below is over 5 classes (kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, cymbals), as in `docs/report.md`.

## Decision
The app and `stickler notate` offer two modes (`stickler.api.MODES`):

- **Fast** (the default): ADTOF on the original mix, every class, with ADTOF's own thresholds.
  About 15 s for a 4-minute song on the laptop's CPU.
- **Quality**: MuScriptor medium on the Demucs drum stem for kick, snare and toms; ADTOF on the
  mix for hi-hat and cymbals. About 14 minutes for a 4-minute song on the laptop's CPU.

## History
- **2026-11-12, two modes** (owner): one fast mode that runs on any laptop, one slower mode for
  the best result.
- **2026-11-13, the modes' contents** (owner, with the 25-song results; release 1). The
  combination was chosen on the first 10 own songs and confirmed on MDB. Tuning on MDB, voting and
  fusion improved MDB but made the own songs worse, so ADTOF keeps its default thresholds (report
  section 6, `tasks/todo.md` Phase 5c). On all 25 own songs: Quality 0.776, Fast 0.770 (better on
  16 songs, worse on 2; mean +0.015, 95 % interval −0.001 to +0.030). Quality was kept because it
  can be clearly better for a particular song. A higher cymbal threshold (0.44) helped on the own
  songs only, so it is not used.
- **2026-11-14, kept after the correction** (owner, release 3). 100 error spots per mode, judged
  blind by ear, showed how many errors are the references': corrected macro-F1 Fast 0.867
  (0.841–0.896), Quality 0.842 (0.818–0.868). Fast is likely ahead, but the intervals overlap.
  Quality stays as a mode for now (report section 5.5).
- **Release 7, new candidates** (SPEC.md "Release 7"): DrumSep with onsets, MuScriptor large and
  Separate-and-Detect, measured on all three datasets (`sad` on the own songs only if it comes
  within 0.03 of MuScriptor large on MDB and ENST). Decision rule: SPEC.md "Release 7", method 6.
- **2026-11-17, both modes unchanged** (owner, release 7). Measured macro-F1 on the 25 own songs:
  Quality 0.776, Fast 0.770, MuScriptor large on the Demucs stem 0.704, DrumSep with onsets on
  the Demucs stem 0.589 (0.568 with thresholds tuned on MDB and ENST). Separate-and-Detect was
  closed on MDB (mean F1 about 0.60 with tuned thresholds against 0.73 and 0.81), so it was not
  run on the own songs. No candidate came within 0.01 of a mode (5 classes, owner), so there was
  no listening round. MuScriptor large is the only one close on some classes: it is slightly
  better than medium at kick and snare (0.898 and 0.899 against 0.895 and 0.896) and worse at
  toms (0.619 against 0.650), hi-hat and cymbals. In place of medium inside Quality it would give
  0.771; large for kick and snare with medium for toms 0.777, a tie at about twice the computing
  time (both models). On ENST drummer 3 it beats medium clearly (0.795 against 0.718 on the
  Demucs stem), on MDB too (0.781 against 0.747), but the own songs decide. Report section 5.6.

## Consequences
- Two modes to test, document and keep working: in the app, the command line and the requirements
  (`docs/requirements.md`).
- Checkpoint C is closed by this ADR: the winning pipeline is the pair of modes, chosen by the
  owner on 2026-11-13 and kept on 2026-11-14.
- A replaced mode is a new decision in this ADR, with its numbers, not a silent config change.
- Release 7 changes nothing in the app's models; the adapters `drumsep_onset`, `muscriptor_l`
  and `sad` stay in the benchmark only.
