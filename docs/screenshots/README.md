# Screenshots

Taken from the app (headless, `scripts/gtk.sh test` style) for the AppStream MetaInfo
(`packaging/ng.tshia.Stickler.metainfo.xml`) and the README, at 1000 × 700.

The README's animated overview `overview.gif` is recorded with
`scripts/gtk.sh demo <path to Fluffing a Duck>` (scripts/demo.py): on an invisible screen with
private settings and an empty results cache, the app opens the song, analyses it with the
checkout's models (Fast)
and steps through it; the analysis plays faster than it ran.

The song is "Fluffing a Duck" by Kevin MacLeod ([incompetech.com](https://incompetech.com)),
licensed under [Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The sheet music on the screenshots is Stickler's transcription of its drums.

| File | Shows |
|---|---|
| `sheet.png` | The sheet music, a bar marked during playback |
| `corrections.png` | The corrections sidebar |
| `dark.png` | The sheet music in the dark style |
| `overview.gif` | Open, analyse, follow the sheet music (800 px wide; recorded on the laptop) |
