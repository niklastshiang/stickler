Installing Stickler on Windows

In short

You need Windows 11 on a PC with an Intel or AMD processor. Download the installer, get past Windows’ warning once, and install. No administrator rights are needed. It takes about two minutes.

Windows warns because Stickler’s installer is not signed yet: signing costs developers a yearly fee, and Stickler is free. A free signature for open-source projects is applied for.

Step by step

  1. Download it

    Choose Download for Windows. The installer, a file ending in -setup.exe, lands in your Downloads folder. If the browser says the file is not commonly downloaded, choose to keep it.

  2. Get past the warning

    Open the downloaded file. Windows shows Windows protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Nothing is wrong: Windows has not seen this new file often enough yet.

    Microsoft explains the warning on its page SmartScreen reputation.

  3. Install

    The installer speaks the language of Windows and asks for no folder and no password. Choose Install, then Finish with Start Stickler ticked. Stickler is now in the Start menu and on the desktop.

The first start

Stickler needs trained models to hear the drums. It shows which ones, their licences and their size, and downloads them only after you agree. After that it works without the internet; your songs stay on your PC.

When Windows says no

Only “Don’t run”, no “Run anyway”: Smart App Control is on. It runs only signed programs, so it starts Stickler once Stickler is signed. Microsoft explains it in Smart App Control: frequently asked questions.

A company PC: its antivirus may hold back a new program the first time it starts (“Access is denied”). Try again half an hour later, or ask your IT department to allow Stickler.

Windows 10, or a PC with an ARM processor: the installer says that Stickler needs Windows 11 with an Intel or AMD processor, and installs nothing.

Removing it again

Choose File › Uninstall Stickler… in the app, or Stickler’s Uninstall in Settings › Apps › Installed apps. Both remove the program, its models and the saved analyses, and ask whether your settings, recent songs and corrections go too. Your songs and exports stay.
