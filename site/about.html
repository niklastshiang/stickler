    How it is made

    Stickler is built with Claude, an AI from Anthropic, writing the code, while the author decides what it does and how it looks. Every change is tested before it counts, and the whole code is open on GitHub under the GPL-3.0-or-later licence.

    Stickler does not train models of its own. It brings together open models from research and measures how well they work on real songs; the numbers are in the project’s report.

    Thanks and licences

    • ADTOF hears the hits; its trained model is free for non-commercial use (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
    • Beat This! finds the beat and the bars (MIT licence).
    • Demucs separates the drum track (MIT licence).
    • Verovio engraves the sheet music (LGPL-3.0) with the Leipzig music font (SIL Open Font License).
    • This site is set in Figtree (SIL Open Font License).
    • The example song is “Fluffing a Duck” by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0.

    Stickler never ships a model: the app downloads each one on your computer after naming its licence.
