Privacy

In short

  • The app keeps your songs on your computer and sends nothing about you anywhere.

  • Its host, GitHub, processes your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • Your rights under the GDPR are in articles 15–21 and 77; ask me anything about this statement.

The app

Stickler works on your computer. Your songs, the sheet music and your settings stay there; nothing is sent anywhere. There is no account, no cloud and no statistics.

The app goes online only to download what it needs, and asks before it does: on the first start the two models and the programs they run with, and the first time you save a drum track, the Demucs model. These come from the Python package servers (pypi.org, download.pytorch.org, github.com) and from the models’ own servers (JKU Linz for Beat This!, Hugging Face for Demucs). Like any download, these servers see your computer’s IP address; I receive none of it.

This website

This site sets no cookies, uses no tracking and loads nothing from other servers: its font, pictures and video come from the site itself. I collect no personal data about you.

It is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes your IP address, which is technically needed to send you the page, and logs it for security, whether or not you have a GitHub account. I do not see these logs. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest is to show you this site when you ask for it. What GitHub does with the data is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If you write to me

You do not need to write to me to use this site or the app. If you do, my mail provider mailbox.org (Germany, its privacy policy) and I process your address and message to answer you, and for nothing else. The legal basis is your consent, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you can withdraw at any time.

Your rights

Under articles 15 to 20 GDPR you have the right of access, to rectification, to erasure, to restriction of processing, to be notified of these, and to data portability. As I process no data that identifies you, these apply only once you give me information that does (Article 11 GDPR).

You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Article 6(1)(e) or (f) GDPR (Article 21 GDPR).

You may also complain to a supervisory authority, in particular in the member state where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place (Article 77 GDPR).
